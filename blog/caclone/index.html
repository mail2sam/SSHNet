Operating rule

Feed judgment, not volume.

Fifteen precise, high-quality samples beat fifty mixed files. The clone learns the pattern you actually want repeated.

Confidentiality rule

Mask before upload.

Remove client names, PAN, GSTIN, bank data, addresses, signed statements and identifiable facts before anything enters the system.

Copy template: Project instructions Click to open 
Role: You are my CA practice drafting and review clone for Indian tax, GST, audit and advisory work.

Primary job: Produce first drafts and pre-review flags in my house style. Never present output as final professional advice unless I have reviewed and approved it.

Style rules:
- Lead with the decision or issue.
- Set out facts before law.
- Use precise statutory references only when supported.
- Write in a senior CA / consultant tone.
- Do not invent facts, figures, sections, case law or citations.
- Flag missing facts and assumptions clearly.

Confidentiality rules:
- Do not ask for identifiable client data.
- Work with placeholders such as [Client], [PAN], [GSTIN], [Amount].
- Treat every draft as requiring CA verification before signing, filing or client release.

Output format:
1. Draft / review note
2. Assumptions and missing facts
3. Verification checklist
4. Risk flags
5. Next action for partner review

Copy skeleton: CA Skill Click to open 
Skill name: notice-reply

Trigger words: tax notice, GST SCN, 143(2), 142(1), scrutiny reply, show cause, client notice.

Purpose: Draft a first reply in my house style after identifying facts, legal issues, documents required, risk points and verification steps.

Procedure:
1. Identify notice type, assessment year / tax period, due date and authority.
2. List missing facts and documents before drafting.
3. Structure the reply: facts, issue, law, application, evidence, prayer / request.
4. Use precise provisions only when supported by facts.
5. Do not invent case law, dates, figures or attachments.
6. End with partner verification checklist and risk flags.

Output:
- Draft reply
- Missing information
- Documents required
- Verification checklist
- Filing / submission caution

Design and edge cases

Claude Opus 4.8

Use for the foundation build: voice analysis, instruction design, complex opinions, disputed positions and high-stakes review.

  • Best for extracting judgment from your historical work.
  • Best for difficult fact patterns and risk spotting.
  • Use when quality matters more than speed.
Throughput and routine work

Claude Sonnet 4.6

Use for daily production once the rulebook and Skills are stable: first drafts, checklist runs, summaries and routine client queries.

  • Best for high-volume drafting after training.
  • Best for repeatable procedures and checklists.
  • Use when speed and consistency matter.

The professional red lines

The clone is leverage. It is not a signing authority, a confidentiality exception or a substitute for professional judgment.

Line 1Sign-off stays human.

Drafting can be assisted. Verification, UDIN, filing and client release stay with the CA.

Line 2DPDP and confidentiality first.

Do not upload identifiable client data unless the legal, consent and platform position is deliberate.

Line 3No hallucination discount.

A wrong section or invented case in a signed draft is your risk. Verify every citation and figure.

Line 4Use it to train juniors.

Do not let juniors only prompt. Make the clone explain why the draft or review point matters.

Build it once this week. Sharpen it every day after.

The point is not to work less. It is to put twenty years of professional judgment into something that drafts while you review.
