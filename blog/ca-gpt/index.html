Good to know

Answers before you open it.

Do I need to learn prompt engineering?

No. Type the task in plain English or paste a notice. The 114 prompts run behind the scenes, so you never write one. If you can describe it to your article assistant, you can ask the GPT.

Where do I find it?

It sits in your ChatGPT sidebar under GPTs, or open it directly with the shared link. Use the "Open the GPT" button anywhere on this page.

Does it file returns or issue a UDIN?

No. It drafts. It will not touch a portal, file a return, or issue a UDIN or certificate. Those stay with you, the practitioner. It prepares, you verify and sign.

How current is the law it uses?

It reasons from the Acts and Rules loaded into it and flags every rate, threshold, due date and section reference for you to confirm against the official source before use.

Can it replace my judgement?

No, and it is not meant to. Every output is a structured draft for your review. AI prepares, you review, amend, sign and own. Judgement stays human.

What can I actually ask it?

Income tax, GST, TDS, MCA and ROC filings, audit, advisory, compliance calendars, FP&A notes and QC, plus client emails, checklists and file notes. Paste a notice or just describe the task.
